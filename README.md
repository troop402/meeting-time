# Meeting Time

👉 **Launch it Here:** [https://troop402.github.io/meeting-time/](https://troop402.github.io/meeting-time/)

A visual meeting timer and agenda display. Run as a website on a laptop, an app on a phone, or cast to a TV over WiFi hands-free. Easy to share and collaborate, no accounts or logins needed.

![Presentation screen for this app](/screens/landscape_presentation.png)

---

## How It Works

The app has two stacked screens:

- **Presentation View (Top Screen):** The big-screen display shown to the room. It displays the current topic, live system clock, notes, what's coming up next, and rotating announcements.
- **Agenda Manager (Bottom Screen):** Scroll down to view the timeline, edit topics, manage announcements, and share the schedule.

[![Management screen for this app](/screens/management_view_thumb.png)](/screens/management_view.png)

---

## Editing Your Agenda

Scroll down to the Agenda Manager to customize your meeting. `[ + Add ]`, edit, or  `[ Delete ]`.

- **Formatting Notes:**
  - Type `- ` for a bullet point.
  - Type `-- ` for an indented sub-bullet.
  - Use `**bold**` or `*italics*` for emphasis.
- Manage **Patrols**, **Dates**, or **Announcements** in the list to update their notes, or toggle the checboxes to choose whether they appear during the meeting.

---

## Sharing an Agenda

There are two easy ways to distribute an agenda:

### 1. Send a Web Link (`[ 🔗 Share ]`)
Click `[ 🔗 Share ]` to copy the link. Anyone opening it sees the exact schedule you created. No accounts or logins.

### 2. Copy or Paste Plain Text (`[ 📋 Import / Edit ]`)
Click `[ 📋 Import / Edit ]` to open the full agenda as plain text. Paste a new agenda into the box and click `[ Save ]` to load it.

---

## Running the Meeting

- The app monitors the real-world clock. When the time reaches the next scheduled item, the presentation advances.
- Tap `[ ⏸ Pause ]`, `[ ◀ Prev ]`, `[ Next ▶ ]`, or click any item on the timeline to take manual control.

---

## Casting to a TV or Projector

You can wirelessly send the presentation to a TV or projector while using your phone as a remote:

- Tap `[ ⚡ Cast ]` and choose your Chromecast or smart TV.
- Put your phone away, the TV continues presenting independently on its own clock.
- Or use the phone to pause, advance slides, or edit details.
